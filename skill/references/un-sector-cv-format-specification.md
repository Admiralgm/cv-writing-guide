# UN Sector CV Format — Reference for AI CV Generation

> Condensed from SearXNG deep research (2026-07-20) across 8 sources: UNESCO Form HR 5-1, UNICEF Careers, UNcareer.net, CLR UN CV Guide, Indeed UN Resume, UNvacancies, UNChannel, Kickresume.

## When to Use This Reference

When generating a CV targeting any UN system organization: UNICEF, UNESCO, ITU, UNDP, WHO, UN Secretariat, WFP, UNHCR, UNOPS, UNFPA, ILO, IAEA, etc.

## Key Difference: UN CV vs Private Sector CV

UN CVs differ from private-sector resumes in several structural ways:
1. **Personal data is more extensive** — nationality (ALL nationalities), DOB, place of birth, marital status, gender, name at birth, nationality at birth (UNESCO Form 250 requires all)
2. **Education comes BEFORE experience** — UN convention places education higher than private sector does
3. **Supervisor name and title required** — UNESCO Form 250 requires "Name and title of your immediate supervisor" for each position
4. **Grade or salary per position** — UNESCO Form 250 requires UN grade (if UN system) or yearly salary (if non-UN)
5. **Declaration statement mandatory** — signed truth certification at bottom
6. **Additional info section** — relatives in UN system, restrictions, criminal record declaration, geographic availability, notice period
7. **References listed on CV** — not "available upon request" — minimum 3 with full contact details
8. **Language proficiency matrix** — Speak/Write/Read/Understand for each language (UNESCO Form 250 format)
9. **British English** — "programme" not "program", "organisation" not "organization"
10. **Date format** — DD/MM/YYYY (European), not MM/DD/YYYY (US)
11. **Longer than private-sector CV** — 3-4 pages acceptable (vs 2-3 for private sector)

## When to Use Agency Form vs Free-Format CV

| Situation | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| UNESCO explicitly requests Form HR 5-1 / Form 250 | Download and fill official .doc form |
| UN Secretariat (careers.un.org / Inspira) | Inspira online P-11/PHP system |
| UNICEF, UNDP, WHO, ITU, most others | Free-format CV following this spec |
| Consultant/Individual Contractor roles | Free-format CV + cover letter |

## Mandatory Section Order (UN Free-Format CV)

1. PERSONAL DATA (name, address, ALL nationalities, contact, DOB, gender, marital status)
2. EDUCATION (reverse chronological, highest first)
3. SUMMARY OF PROFESSIONAL SKILLS AND CAPABILITIES (areas of expertise, certifications, IT skills)
4. PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE / EMPLOYMENT RECORD (reverse chronological, most recent first)
5. PUBLICATIONS (max 4, omit if none)
6. LANGUAGES (mother tongue + additional with Speak/Write/Read/Understand proficiency)
7. REFERENCES (minimum 3, full contact details — NOT "available upon request")
8. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION (relatives in UN, restrictions, geographic availability, notice period)
9. CERTIFICATION / DECLARATION (signed truth statement)

## UNESCO Form HR 5-1 (Form 250) — Additional Fields Not in Free-Format CV

- Name at birth
- Nationality at birth
- Legal steps (name/status changes)
- Gender
- Marital status
- Secondary education (if highest qualification)
- Name and title of immediate supervisor (for EACH position)
- Grade if UN system / yearly salary if non-UN (for EACH position)
- Professional societies memberships
- Relatives/spouse employed in UN system (name, relationship, organization)
- Criminal record declaration (Yes/No + explanation)
- Objection to employer enquiry (Yes/No)
- Willingness to serve in any region (Yes/No, if no which and why)
- Notice period
- Certification: "I certify that the answers made by me to the foregoing questions and all information provided in support of my candidature are true and complete."

## Agency-Specific CV Tips

- **UNICEF:** Highlight public health, education, child protection, social policy. Reference UNICEF Competency Framework. Group duties into categories, not long bullet lists. Use vacancy's exact terminology.
- **UNESCO:** Lead with education/science/culture experience. If Form 250 requested, fill ALL fields (use "N/A" if not applicable).
- **ITU:** Emphasize telecom/ICT background. Reference ITU-T, ITU-R, ITU-D sectors. Technical certifications carry premium weight.
- **UNDP:** Emphasize sustainable development, SDGs, capacity building. Experience in developing countries/fragile states valued.
- **WHO:** Emphasize public health, health systems. Medical/health qualifications carry premium weight. English + French are working languages.
- **UN Secretariat:** Use Inspira system. Uploaded CV is secondary to the Inspira profile.

## Formatting Rules (UN-Specific)

- Page: A4, 2.5cm margins, Calibri/Arial/Times New Roman 11pt
- No tables, no graphics, no columns, no text boxes (ATS compatibility)
- British English spelling: programme, organisation, centre, behaviour, favour
- Date format: DD/MM/YYYY (European/UN standard)
- No photograph (UN agencies explicitly do NOT require photos)
- UN grade notation: P-1 through P-5, D-1, D-2, G-1 through G-7, NO-A through NO-D, FS-1 through FS-7
- "Duty station" not "office" or "location"

## Declaration Statement (Include on All UN CVs)

```
I CONFIRM THAT ALL INFORMATION STATED IN THIS RESUME IS CORRECT AND COMPLETE
TO THE BEST OF MY KNOWLEDGE. I ALLOW THE UNITED NATIONS TO VALIDATE THE
INFORMATION PROVIDED IN THIS RESUME.

Applicant's Signature: _______________________
Applicant's Name: [FULL NAME]
Date: [DD/MM/YYYY]
```
