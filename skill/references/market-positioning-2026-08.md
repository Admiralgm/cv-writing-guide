## What "hands-on" means for positioning

"Hands-on" appears 63× across profiles — the deepest consistent signature.
User is the executive who does not delegate the hard part. This is the brand,
and it is rare: most AI talent cannot sit in a board meeting; most executives
cannot build the system.

## Market value (2026)

Scarce combination, in rising demand:
1. **AI-builder-executive hybrid** — architect agentic systems AND justify ROI
   at board level. Most marketable identity; 106 CVs already lead with it.
2. **Emerging-market delivery credibility** — Africa/Middle East field rollout
   scars (2008–2014) are unfakeable; directly on-thesis for UNICEF Giga, IFIs,
   telcos, edtech deploying in the Global South.
3. **UN system fluency** — knows how UNICEF/WHO/FAO/UNOPS/ILO actually operate;
   127 CVs target this world; UN is hiring aggressively for AI roles.
4. **EU passport + Russian** — Czech citizenship = unrestricted EU labor market
   (+NATO/OECD); Russian opens CIS/EAEU. Rare mobility combination.
5. **Telecom/fintech infrastructure depth** (MVNO/MVNE, voice, payments,
   connectivity) — the physical layer the AI economy runs on.

## The honest warning (chameleon risk)

Corpus shows applications as CTO, COO, AI Director, TPM, consultant, product
lead, GM — sometimes within the same month. Recruiters read this spread as
either "rare generalist executive" or "unfocused." The market pays most for ONE
identity. The strongest, most current, most provable framing:

**"AI-enabled operations and transformation executive with telecom,
emerging-market, and UN depth"** — the AI_Operations_Transformation and EPAM
Director positioning. Use this as the default lens; vary titles, not identity.
